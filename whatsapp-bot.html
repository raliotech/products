Privacy Policy – Ralio WhatsApp Assistant

Effective Date: 02/10/2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Ralio Technologies LLP ("Ralio", "we") handles information when you message the Ralio WhatsApp assistant. By sending a message to our WhatsApp number, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Receive

When you message us, WhatsApp (operated by Meta) delivers the following to us:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The content of your messages, including any text, images, documents or audio you send.
  • Message metadata such as timestamps and delivery status.

We only receive messages you choose to send us. We do not access your contacts, other chats or anything else on your phone.

3. How We Use It

  • To read and reply to your messages.
  • To keep conversation context so replies make sense.
  • To troubleshoot problems and keep the service secure.

Messages are processed on servers operated by Ralio. Automated replies are generated by AI models that run on our own servers; your messages are not sent to third-party AI providers.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Conversation data is stored on Ralio's servers and included in our internal backups.
  • We keep conversation data for up to 90 days, after which it is deleted, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising or marketing. Messages pass through WhatsApp's infrastructure, which is governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections between WhatsApp and our servers. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • You can stop using the assistant at any time by no longer messaging it, or by blocking the number in WhatsApp.
  • To have your data deleted, email us at the address below from any address, include the WhatsApp number you used, and write "Delete my data". We will delete your conversation data within 30 days and confirm by email.

8. Children

The assistant is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new Effective Date.
